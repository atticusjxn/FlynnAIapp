## 1. The scoreboard — winning metrics

These are the only numbers that matter before you raise or scale. Hit the bars and the rest of this doc unlocks itself.

| Metric | Why it's the one | Target bar |
|---|---|---|
| **Week-4 unprompted retention** | The single number investors index on. Do operators *initiate* Flynn for a recurring job after 4 weeks, without a reminder? | **≥ 30%** of activated users still active weekly |
| **Activation rate** (signup → first real win) | Your untested onboarding lives or dies here. | **≥ 35%** |
| **Time-to-first-value** | Wispr/Lovable lesson: instant wow drives conversion. | **Minutes**, on the receipt loop |
| **Free → paid conversion** | Proves the paywall lands at the value moment. | **5–10%+** |
| **CAC per activated user** | Tells you if paid can ever scale. | **< $20 AUD** |
| **MoM growth** | The up-and-to-the-right line. | **20–30%+** |
| **Cost-to-serve / active user** | Flynn is agentic — protect margin. | **< 40% of price** |
| **LTV : CAC** | The verdict on whether paid is a real engine. | **≥ 3 : 1** |

**The one chart to build:** a retention curve that *flattens* (a cohort that keeps coming back), not one that decays to zero. That flattening is what makes an investor lean in.

## 4. The ads — exact spec

**Format:** 9:16 vertical video, screen-recordings of the actual text thread. The product *is* a text conversation, so the demo is inherently native to the feed and legible in 3 seconds. Show the agent **actually working** — that pre-qualifies the swipe so people who tap already get it.

**How many:** start with **5 creatives**, one hook each. Kill the bottom 3 fast, pour spend into the 1–2 winners.

| # | What it shows | Hook | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Snap a servo/materials receipt → Flynn texts back the logged sheet | "POV: you photographed one receipt and your books did themselves" | **Conversion** (instant wow) |
| 2 | "Chase the Jones invoice" → Flynn drafts + sends the follow-up | "That $2,400 nobody's paid in 3 weeks? Flynn just chased them for you" | **Pain** (get-paid) |
| 3 | One text, three tasks (chase X, invoice Y, include the part, here's the receipt) | "I sent this AI one message and it did three things at once" | **"It's an agent"** (killer demo) |
| 4 | Voice note → Flynn executes | "Hate admin? Don't text. Just talk to it." | **Friction-killer** (texting objection) |
| 5 | Sunday-night digest + one-tap actions | "While you had a beer, your admin sorted itself" | **Stickiness** (proactive) |

**Lead with #1 and #3.** #1 wins the swipe on instant value; #3 proves it's a real agent, not a single-trick app.

**Budget:**
- **Pre-flight:** $80–100/day × ~5 days = **~$450–500**, watched daily.
- **During Europe:** continue **$30/day** *only if* pre-flight clears the bar (CAC/activated < $20 + early repeat use). Treat this as *seeding*, not scaling — it'll run unoptimised while you can't touch it.
- **Hold the rest** for post-Europe, when the funnel's dialled and you're back at the wheel.

**Targets:**
- **CPC:** aim **< $1 AUD** (you've seen $0.30 on Mates Rates; $0.70–$2 is the honest cold band). Expect it to start high and improve as conversion events accumulate.
- **Click → texts Flynn:** 50–65%
- **Texts → activated:** 35%+ *(the make-or-break dial — watch it)*
- **Activated → regular:** 40%+
- **Regular → paying:** 30%+

**Traffic destination:** cold ad traffic → a **landing page** (frames value, lets you retarget). Keep the **direct deep-link-to-text** for warm/Mates-Rates traffic who already get it.

## 5. Pricing & the Stripe link

**Launch price: $49/month AUD.** Anchored below the real alternative (an admin/bookkeeper is $50k+/yr), in line with ServiceM8-tier tools. Test $39 vs $49 once you have volume — don't discount below that early; cheap-then-discounted trains people to undervalue it.

**Framing:** "free to start," never just "free." Flynn soft-signals the trial early ("you're on the free trial") so the wall is expected, not bait-and-switch.

**The paywall mechanic (yours, and it's right):** Flynn keeps *chatting* but stops *doing*. It fires a Stripe payment link (Apple Pay enabled) **at the moment of want**, with the unfinished task as the hook: *"Want me to finish chasing that invoice? Tap to unlock — $49/mo."* Pay → instant text: *"Sorted. Want me to pick up where we left off?"*

**The trigger — convert on fast value, retain on slow.** Don't wait the full get-paid cycle to charge. Paywall after the *instant* wins (a couple of receipt logs, a sent invoice). The receipt loop earns the $49; the invoice-chasing and digest are what keep them paying. Underneath, meter by usage so power users (complex tasks, higher serve cost) hit the wall exactly when they're most hooked — but show them a simple flat price, never credits.

## 6. Unit-economics targets

Plug real numbers into the calculator as the test produces them. The two dials that swing everything: **activation rate** and **cost-to-serve**. The model, not the CPC, decides if paid scales.

- Realistic blended serve cost (Qwen Flash on chat + a stronger model on money-touching tasks + browserbase + OCR + relay): **~$8–20/active user/mo** — *not ~$0*. "Free so far" is the expiring Alibaba trial, not a permanent price.
- Route models by stakes: cheap/fast for casual chat, accurate/stronger for anything that touches a customer's invoice or books. A wrong invoice kills retention, which kills the LTV the whole model rests on. Pick on task-success rate, not sticker price.

## 7. North star — the 3–5 year unicorn vision

**"The front office for every small business — lands with one operator, spreads through the whole crew, and eventually runs the back office itself."**

The ladder (each rung earns the next):

1. **Year 1 — own AU solo tradies.** Prove retention + cheap CAC on the receipt/invoice wedge. Raise pre-seed/accelerator from that proof.
2. **Year 1–2 — adjacent AU verticals.** Salons, PTs, real estate, freelancers. Same four triggers (lead reply, reminder, invoice chase, quote chase), different surface. TAM expands with near-zero product change.
3. **Year 2–3 — geography + teams.** UK then US tradies (10× the market). And the **multiplayer rung**: a solo user who loves Flynn drags his apprentice/partner into the thread when he grows — the individual is the trojan horse, expansion is organic and per-seat. (This is the Granola/Lovable land-and-expand shape.)
4. **Year 3–5 — the operating layer for small business.** Solo → crews → the front office that replaces the *Jobber/Xero front-end*, then absorbs the back office itself: at scale, the unit economics let Flynn run the invoicing and books in-house, so operators never need Xero. **Start as the interface, end as the full stack.**

**The moat that compounds:** every conversation accumulates proprietary task-trajectory data — what jobs, in whose voice, what converts — training specialist models that make Flynn the system of record, not a wrapper. Long-term, owned infra (the smaller Qwen models are Apache 2.0 → self-hostable, which also solves data-residency for enterprise).

## 8. Why Flynn is something investors throw money at — the Lovable & Granola lessons

Both grew with **~$0 paid acquisition** and became multi-billion-dollar stories. Here's the pattern, and how Flynn already fits it.

**Lovable** ($0 → $100M ARR in 8 months, ~$400M in 14, $6.6B valuation, no ad spend):
- *Founder-brand as distribution* — Anton built an audience first. **→ You have @atticusjxn.** This is your version, and it's a real asset, not a vanity metric.
- *PLG paywall at the invested moment* — free with a cap, upgrade prompt the instant you're hooked. **→ Your "stop doing, keep chatting, tap to unlock" mechanic is exactly this.**
- *Solve the one hard blocker in one click* (Supabase backend). **→ Your one-link onboarding must be that frictionless. It's the thing to nail.**

**Granola** ($1.5B valuation, word-of-mouth growth, ~10%/week):
- *Spread by word of mouth in a tight community* (VCs/founders). **→ Tradies are your community — they talk, and word travels in trades faster than any ad.**
- *"Where they live all day"* — it became the home base. **→ Flynn lives in iMessage, where tradies already are all day. Same insight.**
- *Single-user → team → enterprise.* **→ Your solo-to-crew expansion, validated by a $1.5B comp.**
- *Passive → active (notes → agent that does tasks).* **→ Flynn is born active. Granola is climbing toward where you already start.**

**The honest implication:** the investable story is **not** "I can buy users with Meta ads." It's "I have an organic distribution engine (content + community), a wedge that lives where users already are, instant time-to-value, and a solo→team land-and-expand with a data moat — and the incumbents can't follow (Meta won't bridge out of its walled garden; ServiceM8/Jobber are logins, not an agent that texts you first)." Ads are to **test and seed**. The growth thesis investors fund is the organic engine. Build that, show the retention curve, and Flynn becomes exactly the shape of company the last two years rewarded.

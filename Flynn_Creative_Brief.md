## 1. What Flynn is (the one-liner)

**Flynn is the AI you text to run your business. No app, no login — it lives in iMessage.**

Service operators text Flynn like a smart mate who knows their business, and Flynn does the admin: logs receipts to their books, drafts and chases quotes, sends and chases invoices, sends a weekly money summary, books jobs, orders parts. Flynn texts back and **executes** — it doesn't just chat or summarise.

**Brand promise (use this line, it's the spine of everything): "Run your business from iMessage."**

## 2. Who it's for

- **Now (the wedge):** Australian solo tradies and micro-teams — sparkies, plumbers, removalists, concreters, cleaners, arborists, PTs, nail techs, any small service operator who runs the business from their phone and has **no dedicated admin person**. They do the paperwork at 11pm or push it off till they're at a computer.
- **The pain (real + quantified):** solo operators lose ~10–15 hours/week to admin (quoting, invoicing, chasing payments, scheduling), mostly after hours. ~44% of AU small-business invoices are paid late. Most still run on texts, memory, and a shoebox of receipts.
- **Later (the TAM):** small/medium teams, then teams with admin staff (Flynn becomes the communication + task layer that structures what the crew sends so nothing falls through the cracks). Don't lead with this — lead with solo.

## 3. What Flynn actually does (all live, nothing is vaporware)

- **Receipt → books (the hero / activation wow):** snap a photo of a receipt → parsed → logged to Google Sheets or Xero → Flynn texts back the result. Instant, zero-trust-cost.
- **Quotes:** drafts a quote you can send, records it, and chases it if it goes cold.
- **Invoices:** sends invoices (Xero), and chases unpaid ones — on your say-so.
- **Weekly money digest:** Sunday summary — "this week: 3 unpaid, 2 quotes still open."
- **Email from YOUR account:** drafts + sends emails/quotes from the operator's own Gmail, Outlook/365, or other provider (Bigpond/iCloud/host) — not a generic bot address.
- **Books jobs** into their calendar; **orders parts** from trade suppliers.
- **Core principle:** Flynn **confirms before anything irreversible** (sending an invoice, an order). It **nudges you to chase**, it never messages a client on its own.

## 4. Why it's different (the moat)

- **No app, your existing number.** Competitors (ServiceM8, Jobber, Tradify) are *systems you log into* — their own marketing admits setup takes days-to-weeks. Flynn is one text. This is the most uncontested thing we can say — deliver it with confidence, never as a defensive "ugh, not another app."
- **It executes in the world** (raises the invoice, chases the payment, books the job) — vs tools that only draft replies inside a chat, or aggregate/summarise your channels (e.g. Kinso). Flynn acts; it doesn't just inform.
- **Lives where they already are.** No new surface, no platform gatekeeper. Meta's business agent talks to *your customers* inside Meta's walls; Flynn does *your* back office and bridges out to your real tools (Xero, Sheets, email, calendar).

No "against the odds" sob story.

## 6. BRAND VOICE — non-negotiable rules

Flynn's messages and the marketing copy must read like a real person, never a product or a marketer.

- **lowercase where natural** ("hey", "got it", "done", "no dramas")
- **NO em dashes (—) ever.** Use commas or short sentences.
- **Contractions always** (I'll, you've, can't).
- **Short sentences.** Two short ones beat one long one.
- **No corporate filler / banned words:** never "Great!", "Absolutely!", "streamline", "leverage", "solution", "platform", "optimise", "elevate", "unlock", "in today's fast-paced world", "boost productivity". Anything that smells American/corporate is out.
- **AU tradie slang that lands:** arvo, knock off, on the tools, the missus, sparky/chippie/brickie/removalist, fair dinkum, no dramas, sort it, get paid, chasing your dough, the books, EOFY, ute, servo, cuppa, maccas.
- **Currency = AUD** (Flynn infers from phone prefix: +61 AUD, +64 NZD, +1 USD).
- **Hooks:** first line must land in ~2 seconds. Provocative is fine, but keep it *on-topic* so it pulls the right people.
- **Two product guardrails to never break in scripts:** (1) Flynn **asks before sending** to a client and nudges *you* to chase — never show it autonomously messaging a customer. (2) Flynn never gives tax advice or calls something "deductible" — keep it "tax-ready / sorted by category / your accountant'll love you."
